Official Business Description — Under 250 Words
"MAVTG (Medical Adventure Voyage Travel Group, LLC) is a Florida-based medical travel company that specializes in trip planning and healthcare navigation for dialysis patients and chronic illness travelers, eliminating the logistical barriers that prevent patients from experiencing the world by coordinating dialysis access, insurance authorizations, and full travel itineraries around treatment schedules. Founded by MM — a six-year dialysis patient, published author, and kidney disease advocate — MAVTG draws on lived patient experience to provide services that generic travel agencies cannot: verified dialysis center bookings, cruise line medical vetting, destination research reports, and emergency protocol preparation, all built by someone who has navigated every obstacle firsthand. Through its consulting services, Moore Legacy Press publishing imprint, adaptive PICC and port-access clothing line, and patient advocacy and speaking work, MAVTG exists to demonstrate that a life without limits is possible even on dialysis — and to build the infrastructure that makes that life accessible for every patient who deserves it."
