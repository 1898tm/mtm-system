👑 Bespoke Bangkok — Your Custom-Made Guide
Bangkok is one of the world's best cities for bespoke everything — suits, shirts, shoes, and leather goods — at a fraction of Western prices. You get luxury quality, made exactly for your body, at 10–20% of what it would cost in the US. With dialysis 3× a week, you have time between sessions to do multiple fittings.
👔 Suits — 2 Custom
Where: Sukhumvit Soi 3–11 tailors (Tailor on Ten, Raja's, Marco Tailor)
Colors: Charcoal gray + Navy only (palette rule)
Fabric: Ask for wool-silk blend — breathable, drapes beautifully, doesn't wrinkle
Timeline: 3–5 days (2 fittings minimum — initial + final)
Cost: $200–$450 each custom. Go mid-range, not cheapest.
🎩 Smoking Jacket
Style: Velvet or heavy brocade. Navy or black (palette only)
Details: Shawl lapel, satin trim, jetted pockets. This is your statement piece.
Where: Same tailor as suits (get them to make a matching set)
Pairs with: White dress shirt + black dress pants or suit trousers
Cost: $100–$250
👟 Bespoke Shoes
Get 2 pairs: Black penny loafers + Stone/tan monk strap
Why bespoke: Measured to your exact foot — critical for comfort during dialysis days
Where: Chatuchak Weekend Market (leather cobblers) or Siam Paragon bespoke
Timeline: 3–5 days
Cost: $80–$180 per pair
👕 Custom Shirts (select)
Only get new ones if: Current custom shirts fall outside the neutral palette
Get: 3–4 white cotton dress shirts (your workhorse), 2 white linen for casual
Details: Spread collar, French cuffs if you want cufflinks. No patterns.
Where: Same tailor as suits for consistency
Cost: $30–$80 each custom
💡 Fitting day strategy: Schedule fittings on dialysis days (you're in the area, you have recovery time). First visit = measurements + fabric selection. Day 3 = initial fitting. Day 5 = final pickup. Always go mid-range with tailors — the cheapest options cut corners on lining, canvas, and thread quality.
